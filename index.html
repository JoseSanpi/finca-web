Es Serval, our familiar finca in Alcudia

More than 30 years ago, our family bought this finca overlooking the Serra de Tramuntana. What used to be a small farmhouse has become, with time, love and a lot of manual labor, a cozy rural villa.

Every corner has been designed by us, taking care of the details and respecting the natural environment. Today, Es Serval is a place to disconnect, enjoy the tranquility and feel at home.

Privileged location

On top of a hill in Son Fé, overlooking the Serra de Tramuntana and only 5 minutes from the beach and Alcudia

Ideal for families

Playgrounds, tranquility, nature and plenty of space: all designed to be enjoyed by the whole family.

Natural environment

15,000 m² with garden, swimming pool, children's area, sports court and a small forest up to the top

Exclusive use

The entire property is for exclusive use. Without sharing spaces, ideal for relaxing in peace and quiet

Services

We offer everything you need to enjoy a relaxing and active stay

Frequently Asked Questions

We solve the most common doubts so you can plan your stay with total peace of mind and enjoy every moment at our finca

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM to 12:00 AM, and check-out is by 10:00 AM.

Is early check-in or late check-out available?

You can request it when booking, but it's subject to availability and can't be guaranteed.

How many guests can stay at the finca?

The house accommodates up to 6 guests comfortably.

Are pets allowed?

Yes! We are pet-friendly. Let us know in advance if you're bringing a furry friend.

Is the pool safe for children?

The swimmingpool is not fenced, so children must be supervised at all times.

How far is the finca from the nearest beach?

The closest beaches (like Playa de Muro and Port d'Alcúdia) are just under 5 km away.

Do I need a car to get around?

Yes, we strongly recommend renting a car to explore the area comfortably, reach the beaches, and enjoy nearby restaurants and attractions with ease.
